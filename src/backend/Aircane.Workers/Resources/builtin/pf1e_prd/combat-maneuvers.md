# Combat Maneuvers

## Combat Maneuver Bonus (CMB)

Some attacks disregard a target's armor, such as grapple or trip. These are called combat
maneuvers. Your Combat Maneuver Bonus represents your ability to perform such maneuvers:

CMB = Base attack bonus + Strength modifier + special size modifier

## Combat Maneuver Defense (CMD)

Each creature has a Combat Maneuver Defense representing its ability to resist maneuvers:

CMD = 10 + Base attack bonus + Strength modifier + Dexterity modifier + special size
modifier

## Performing a Maneuver

To perform a combat maneuver, make an attack roll and add your CMB. If the result equals
or exceeds the target's CMD, the maneuver succeeds. Common maneuvers include bull rush,
disarm, grapple, overrun, sunder, and trip. Attempting a maneuver usually provokes an
attack of opportunity unless you have a feat or ability that says otherwise.
