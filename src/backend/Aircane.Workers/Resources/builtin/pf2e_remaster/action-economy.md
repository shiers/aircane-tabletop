# Encounter Rules

Summarized from the Pathfinder Second Edition Remaster rules (ORC License).

## The Three-Action Economy

On your turn during an encounter, you gain 3 actions and 1 reaction. You can spend your
actions in nearly any combination: move three times, attack three times, or mix movement,
attacks, and other activities. Most basic things a character does cost a single action,
which is represented by one action symbol.

Some activities take more than one action to complete and list the number of actions they
require. A reaction has a trigger and can be used even when it isn't your turn, but you
only get one reaction per round.

## Free Actions

A free action doesn't cost you any of your actions per turn. Some free actions have a
trigger, like a reaction, but most can be used at will as long as it is your turn.

## The Multiple Attack Penalty

The more attacks you make beyond your first in a single turn, the less accurate you become.
The second attack on your turn takes a -5 penalty, and the third and subsequent attacks
take a -10 penalty. Agile weapons reduce these penalties to -4 and -8.

## Initiative

At the start of an encounter, everyone involved rolls for initiative to determine the
order in which they act. Usually this is a Perception check, though some situations call
for a different skill.
